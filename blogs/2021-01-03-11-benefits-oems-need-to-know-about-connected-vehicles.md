The automotive industry is changing rapidly. With the introduction of IoT technology and data monetization opportunities, all OEMs now turn their focus on connected vehicles. The aim is to provide a better driver experience and increase safety on the roads, while at the same time finding new ways to increase profits and a continuous monetization.
